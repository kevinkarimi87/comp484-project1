    Protagonist

    Little Mac is the underdog protagonist who serves as the player-controlled boxer.
    Profile: 0–0 (0 KO) | From Bronx, N.Y. | Age: 17 | Weight: 107
    The Coach: Doc Louis is a former heavyweight boxing champion and Little Mac's trainer, who stands in Mac's corner during fight. Between rounds, Doc provides Mac with advice, encouragement, or sometimes a humorous anecdote.

    Opponents

    Minor Circuit

    Glass Joe (Ranked: #2) is extremely weak and has very poor defense. He is mostly in the game for players to learn how to use punches, avoid attacks, and to soften up the experience since even though he towers over them, he's still a really easy fighter to beat.
    Profile: 1–99 (1 KO) | From Paris, France | Age: 38 | Weight: 110

    Von Kaiser (Ranked: #1) has punches that are noticeably faster than Glass Joe's and his defenses are much better, along with the fact that his punches do much more damage.
    Profile: 23–13 (10 KO) | From Berlin, Germany | Age: 42 | Weight: 144

    Piston Honda (Champion) has a weak defense but is much improved when compared to Glass Joe or Von Kaiser. His jab and uppercuts hit hard, and he has a series of rotating left and right jabs called the "Piston Punch".
    Profile: 26–1 (18 KO) | From Tokyo, Japan | Age: 28 | Weight: 174
